# Hermes

Hermes is a personal assistant app used only by its owner, Simon Lu, on his own computer. No one else can sign in to it.

## Privacy

Hermes reads Simon's own Google mail, calendar and files to help him with his day. That data stays on his computer and with the services he already uses. It is never shared, sold or used for anything else.

Questions: open an issue on [this site's repository](https://github.com/Simonl07/simonl07.github.io).
